Common questions

A link won't summarise

Findable reads the public text at a link — a caption, description or auto-generated captions. If a post has none of those, or the platform blocks the request, there is nothing to summarise and the app says so rather than inventing one. The item is still saved, and a Your Notes box appears on it: type or paste what the post said, tap Summarize from my notes, and the summary, action steps, recipe or workout are built from your words instead.

I've run out of AI actions for today

Every summary, recipe, workout and library question uses one AI action. Each plan has a daily allowance that resets at midnight UTC. The profile panel shows what today's actions were spent on. Findable Pro raises the limit.

How do I cancel Findable Pro?

Subscriptions are billed by the store, so they are cancelled there. On iOS: Manage your subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments and subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are the store's too — Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google at Google Play refunds.

How do I delete my account?

Open the profile panel in the app and choose Delete account. It permanently removes your saved items, notes, generated plans and sign-in record straight away. The Privacy Policy explains the two narrow exceptions.

Is my saved content private?

Yes. Your library is only visible to your account. There is no feed, no sharing, no advertising and no third-party analytics in the app.

Should I trust the summaries?

Treat them as a fast recap, not as fact. AI output can be wrong, and it is never medical, financial or safety advice — check the original before acting on anything that matters.
