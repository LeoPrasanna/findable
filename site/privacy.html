Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Findable saves links you send it, reads what is publicly available at that link, and writes a short summary. This page describes exactly what we store to do that, who else touches it, and how to get rid of it.

The short version.
  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app, at any time.

1. Who we are

Findable ("we", "us") is the operator of the Findable mobile app. For any privacy question or request, email savehere.support@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Account information

You sign in with Google or Apple. We never see a password and we never ask you to create one. From the provider we receive only your email address and, where you allow it, your name — the minimum needed to make the account yours. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only ever hold the relay address and the app works exactly the same.

A legacy email-and-password sign-in exists for early test accounts and is being retired. Where it is used, the password is handled by our authentication provider and stored only as a salted hash — we never see it.

Optionally, a first name, last name, nickname and a chosen emoji avatar. These are used only to address you in the app. Leave them blank and the app still works.

Content you save

When you save a link, we store:

  • the link itself and which platform it came from;
  • public information published at that link — title, thumbnail image URL, the creator's public username, duration, and the post's caption or auto-generated captions/transcript;
  • the AI-generated summary, tags and category we derive from it;
  • anything you add yourself — personal notes, and any action steps, recipes, workouts or trip itineraries you ask the app to generate.

When you share a link to Findable from another app, the link is sent to our server in the background and saved without Findable having to open. That is the whole feature; it sends the link and your account's save credential, and nothing else from your phone. You can switch it off under Silent share in the app, and shares will open Findable instead.

We do not download or store the video or audio itself. We read publicly available text. We have no access to your private accounts on Instagram, YouTube, TikTok or anywhere else, and we never ask you to log in to them.

Usage information

A per-day count of how many AI actions you have used, so we can enforce the daily limit on your plan. Alongside it we keep a short, human-readable list of what those actions were (for example a saved item's title, or a truncated version of a question you asked your library) so the app can show you where your daily allowance went. That list is deleted automatically after 2 days.

Crash and error reports

When the app or our server hits an error, a report goes to Sentry so we can fix it: app version, device model, OS version and a stack trace. Personal-data attachment is switched off, so a report carries no profile fields and no saved content. It is diagnostics, not analytics — nothing in it follows you anywhere.

What we do not collect

No advertising identifiers. No location. No contacts, photos or health data. No analytics or attribution SDKs — we do not measure your behaviour in the app, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so we do not need to ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.

3. Why we are allowed to process it

If you are in the UK, EEA or another region with similar law, our legal bases are: performance of a contract (running the account and the save/summarise features you asked for), legitimate interests (keeping the service secure, enforcing usage limits, and preventing abuse of free trials), and consent where you volunteer optional profile details.

4. Who else processes your data

We use a small number of service providers. They process data on our instructions only. We do not sell data to anyone, and we have no advertising partners.

When you save a link, our server also makes an ordinary public request to the source platform (for example YouTube or Instagram) to read that page — the same request any browser would make. Your identity is not sent with it.

We may also disclose data where we are legally required to, or where it is necessary to investigate abuse or protect the safety of users.

5. Where data is stored

Our database and servers are hosted in the United States, and our providers may process data in other countries. Where personal data is transferred out of the UK or EEA, our providers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent approved transfer mechanism.

6. How long we keep it

  • Saved items, notes and generated content — until you delete them, or until you delete your account.
  • Account and profile — until you delete your account.
  • AI action history — 2 days.
  • Daily usage counters — retained after account deletion. They hold only a user identifier, a date and a number, and deleting them would let the daily limit be reset by repeatedly wiping data.
  • Free-trial record — we keep a one-way cryptographic hash of your email address so that the same person cannot claim an unlimited number of free trials. It cannot be reversed into your email address, and it is not used for any other purpose. It is retained after account deletion, because that is the only thing that makes it work.
  • Extraction cache — the public text we read from a link is cached against that link so the same link never has to be fetched twice. It is keyed by the URL and contains no information about who saved it. It is not deleted with your account, because it is not linked to you.

7. Your rights and how to use them

Deletion. Open the profile panel in the app and choose Delete account. This immediately and permanently removes your saved items, notes, generated tasks, workouts and itineraries, your profile, your AI action history, and your sign-in record. It cannot be undone. The two exceptions are listed in section 6.

Access, correction, portability, objection and restriction. Depending on where you live you may have these rights under the GDPR, UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act. Email savehere.support@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a right.

If you are in the UK or EEA and are unhappy with our response, you may complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Every request is checked against the signed token from your sign-in provider and scoped to your account, so one account cannot read another's data. No system is perfectly secure, but we do not store payment details and we never hold a password of yours at all.

9. Children

Findable is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old to use it (16 in the EEA and the UK where required by local law). We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how we handle your data, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
